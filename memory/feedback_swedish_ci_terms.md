In Swedish replies, say "runner"/"runners" (Forgejo/GitHub Actions), not "löpare"/"löparna".

**Why:** Anders finds the Swedish translation silly; established English CI terms read better.

**How to apply:** Keep established English technical terms untranslated in Swedish text (runner, workflow, job, secret where natural).
